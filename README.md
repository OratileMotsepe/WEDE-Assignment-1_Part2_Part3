##Project Overview
Soundmax is a small car audio business based in Pretoria that specializes in the sale and installation of sound systems and electronic equipment in vehicles. This business was created with the fondness of sound waves hitting your eardrums. The vision of the business is to make quality audio systems for all sound and music lovers no matter the budget. 

##Website Goals and Objectives
The main aim of creating the website is to improve the sale of products. A way of doing this is gaining the trust of potential clients by showcasing past work, services provided, and expertise in this field. The website must increase the conversion rate and foot traffic for the business.
