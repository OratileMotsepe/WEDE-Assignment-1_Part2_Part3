About SoundMax

SoundMax is a car audio shop that specializes in custom enclosures and aftermarket audio equipment. We provide sound installations, rewiring and custom enclosures built from scratch. This is our passion and expertise.

Our vision and mission

Our daily operational goal is to create quality audible systems for all types of customers. No matter the budget at hand, we strive to deliver beyond what is expected from us. Seeing clients' reactions to what we are truly capable of is our addiction and for us there is no better thrill than that.
